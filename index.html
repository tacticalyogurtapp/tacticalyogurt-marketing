// features

Everything your farm needs.
Nothing it doesn't.

Purpose-built for print farm operators who need more than a shared spreadsheet.

Inventory Management
Filament Tracking
Every spool tracked by brand, material, color, weight remaining, cost, and location. Progress bars show remaining percentage at a glance.
Hardware Inventory
Screws, inserts, bearings, and small parts tracked by category, spec, quantity, and cost per unit.
Printer Spare Parts
Nozzles, build plates, belts, thermistors, and 16 other part categories — tracked separately with printer compatibility mapping.
Low Stock Alerts
Automatic detection when any item drops below its threshold. Configurable globally and per-item.
Barcode / QR Scanning
Scan a spool's barcode to auto-fill details from a learned product database. Hardware gets QR codes (HW-{id}) for quick lookup.
Stock Audit Trail
Full ledger of every addition, removal, and adjustment with timestamps and user attribution. Undo support for mistakes.
Printer Fleet Management
Printer Profiles
Name, model, nozzle specs, bed dimensions, enclosure status, multi-material capability, and compatible materials per printer.
ROI Tracking
Purchase price, revenue generated, payoff progress bar, and estimated payoff date per printer. Know when each machine pays for itself.
Quick Start Profiles
Pre-built specs for Bambu Lab, Prusa, Creality, Snapmaker, and other popular printers — setup in seconds.
Parts Compatibility
Link specific spare parts to specific printers so you always know what fits what before you order.
Print Job Workflow
Job Logging
Record which file printed on which printer using which spools — with start/end times and outcome tracking (success, partial, failure).
Smart Recommendations
The system ranks available spools and printers by material match, stock level, and past ratings for every job.
Spool Weight Check-In
After a job, enter gross spool weight. The system subtracts tare to automatically update remaining filament.
Job Ratings
Rate each job 1–5 stars on quality, filament performance, and dimensional accuracy. Feeds the recommendation engine over time.
Print File Management
3MF Parsing
Upload .3mf files and auto-extract printer profile, filament requirements per extruder slot, estimated duration, and usage in grams.
Version Tracking
Upload updated versions of the same file. Deduplication by SHA-256 hash ensures no duplicates slip through.
Bill of Materials
Link which printed parts and hardware items each file produces or requires, with quantities per run.
Cost Calculation
Filament + hardware + labor + printer time = total cost per print. Know your margin before you hit start.
Orders & Customer Management
Order Tracking
Orders from Shopify, Etsy, WooCommerce, Square, or manual entry. Status flows from new through fulfilled.
Customer Records
Full order history and total spend per customer. Identified by external platform ID — no PII stored.
Revenue Attribution
Fulfilled order revenue flows back to the printer that produced the parts, feeding per-machine ROI calculation.
Parts Pricing & MSRP
Cost-based MSRP auto-calculated with per-part markup overrides. Dashboard shows total potential revenue across all stock.
Tasks, Markets & Subscriptions
Auto-Generated Tasks
Low stock filament, hardware, and overdue jobs surface automatically. Manual tasks with due dates and user assignment also supported.
Market Event Tracking
Track craft fairs and pop-up shops with dates, location, vendor fees, packing wishlists, and quick order creation.
Creator Subscriptions
Track Patreon, MyMiniFactory, and Cults3D subscriptions with cost and associated files. Profitability leaderboard shows ROI per creator.
Reporting & Analytics
Filament usage, hardware consumption, labor hours, and subscription costs — filterable by date range, material, brand, or item.
